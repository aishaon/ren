Process of Membership

Step-by-step guide to becoming a member of Rangpur Entrepreneurs Network

1

Online Application

Fill out the registration form on our Member Registration page. Provide accurate business and personal information.

2

Email Verification

Check your email and click the verification link to confirm your email address. This activates your account.

3

Payment (if applicable)

If you selected a paid plan, complete the payment through bKash, Nagad, or bank transfer. Basic membership is free.

4

Profile Review

Our team reviews your business profile within 24-48 hours. You may be contacted for additional information.

5

Approval & Welcome

Once approved, you receive a welcome email with your member ID. You can now access all member features and start networking.

6

Active Membership

Congratulations! You are now an active member. Update your profile, list products, and connect with other entrepreneurs.

Processing Time

  • • Basic Membership: Instant (after email verification)
  • • Paid Membership: Within 24-48 hours after payment
  • • Business Verification: 2-5 business days

Documents Required

  • • Valid National ID (NID) or Passport
  • • Trade License (for registered businesses)
  • • Business TIN Certificate
  • • Profile photo (for directory listing)
