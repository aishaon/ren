# Rangpur Entrepreneurs Network (REN)

Rangpur Entrepreneurs Network (REN) is a collaborative platform based in the Rangpur division of Bangladesh that aims to empower and connect local entrepreneurs. It serves as a hub where business owners, startups, freelancers, and aspiring entrepreneurs can come together to share ideas, build partnerships, and grow their ventures.

REN focuses on entrepreneurship development, business networking, and skill enhancement. Through workshops, seminars, and community events, it helps members improve their knowledge in areas like marketing, finance, digital business, and innovation.

One of the key goals of REN is to create a supportive ecosystem where entrepreneurs can promote their products and services, find potential clients or partners, and access new business opportunities. The network also encourages collaboration between experienced business leaders and new entrepreneurs to foster mentorship and guidance.

In addition, REN aims to leverage digital platforms by building a multi-vendor online marketplace, allowing its members to sell their products or services online and expand their reach beyond local markets.

Overall, Rangpur Entrepreneurs Network is dedicated to strengthening the regional economy by supporting entrepreneurship, encouraging innovation, and creating sustainable business growth opportunities.
