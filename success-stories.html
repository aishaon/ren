Empowering Women Through Fashion

Fatema Begum — Fashion House

"As a woman entrepreneur in Rangpur, I faced many challenges. REN gave me the platform and confidence to showcase my designs. The marketplace helped me reach customers beyond my local area."

Fatema now employs 25 women artisans and exports traditional Bengali dresses to Dhaka and international markets.

Modernizing Agriculture in Rangpur

Kamal Hossain — Green Valley Farms

"REN's training programs taught me about modern farming techniques and digital marketing. I now sell organic produce directly to consumers through the marketplace, cutting out middlemen."

Green Valley Farms has grown from 2 acres to 15 acres, supplying organic vegetables to 300+ families weekly.
