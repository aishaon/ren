About Rangpur Entrepreneurs Network

Empowering entrepreneurs since 2020

Our Story

Rangpur Entrepreneurs Network (REN) was established in 2020 with a vision to create a unified platform for entrepreneurs in the Rangpur division. We believe in the power of community and digital transformation.

What started as a small group has grown into a network of 500+ members, including vendors, service providers, and business owners from various sectors.

Our mission is to provide every entrepreneur in Rangpur with the tools, resources, and network they need to succeed.

Our Mission

To empower every entrepreneur in Rangpur with access to digital tools, marketplace opportunities, and a supportive community.

Our Vision

To become the leading entrepreneurial ecosystem in Northern Bangladesh.

Our Core Values

Community First

We believe in togetherness and mutual support.

Innovation
