Our Journey

Empowering traditional businesses with modern tools.

The Rangpur Entrepreneurs Network (REN) was born out of a simple need: to bridge the gap between talented local producers and the growing digital economy.

Today, we serve as a central hub for hundreds of entrepreneurs, providing them with the visibility, tools, and community support they need to thrive in a competitive market.
