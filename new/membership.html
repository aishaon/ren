Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade your plan at any time from your dashboard. If you downgrade, your new rate will apply from the next billing cycle.

How does verification work?

After applying for the Verified Elite plan, our team will conduct a manual check of your business documents and physical location to ensure trust.

What are the marketplace fees?

Basic and Premium members pay a standard 10% platform fee on sales. Verified Elite members enjoy a reduced rate of 7%.
