5 Ways to Scale Your Local Handicraft Business

Md. Rahim

Founder, REN

Scaling a small business in the traditional handicraft sector requires a delicate balance between preserving authenticity and embracing modern digital tools. In the Rangpur division, we are seeing a resurgence of interest in local crafts, but many producers struggle to reach a wider audience.

1. Embrace Digital Storytelling

Your customers aren't just buying a product; they are buying a piece of Rangpur's heritage. Use high-quality photography and video to show the process behind your craft. Whether it's the rhythmic clicking of a loom or the careful painting of pottery, these stories build value and trust.

2. Leverage the REN Marketplace

Instead of building your own e-commerce site from scratch, use the REN platform to list your items. We handle the technical infrastructure, search optimization, and community trust, allowing you to focus on what you do best: creating.

"The future of local business is hybrid. Offline quality meets online reach. This is the foundation upon which REN was built."

As we continue to grow the Rangpur Entrepreneurs Network, our mission remains clear: to provide the digital foundation for your success. We hope these tips help you take the next step in your business journey.
