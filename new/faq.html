Membership & Joining

How do I join the network?

You can sign up through our registration page. Choose a plan that fits your business, fill out your details, and you're ready to go!

What documents are needed for verification?

For Elite verification, we typically require a valid NID, trade license (if applicable), and a physical verification of your business premises.

Selling on Marketplace

What is the platform fee?

We charge a standard 10% commission on every sale made through the platform. This covers technical maintenance and marketing.

How do I get paid?

Once a customer receives their order, your funds enter a 7-day clearance period. After that, you can request a payout to your bKash, Nagad, or Bank account.
