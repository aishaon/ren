Professional Standards

The REN Code of Conduct.

Ensuring a high-integrity ecosystem for North Bengal's most visionary entrepreneurs.

Radical Professionalism

All interactions within the REN network, whether in the community forum or marketplace communications, must adhere to the highest standards of professional courtesy. We value clear, honest, and respectful dialogue.

Quality & Authenticity

Vendors are responsible for ensuring that all marketplace listings are accurate and authentic. Misrepresentation of materials, origins, or production processes is strictly prohibited and results in immediate membership review.

Collaborative Growth

The REN network is built on the principle of collaborative intelligence. Members are encouraged to share insights and support peers. Predatory business practices against other members are not tolerated.

Reporting Violations

If you encounter conduct that violates these standards, please report it to our governance board.
